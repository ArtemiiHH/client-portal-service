# Broker Client Portal UX Audit & Improvement Roadmap

## Service Idea
A fixed-fee B2B consulting service helping **Forex/CFD brokers** identify usability issues in their client portals and turn them into **prioritized, development-ready improvements**. The service complements existing product, design, and development teams rather than replacing them.

## Target Customers
- Small and mid-sized Forex/CFD brokers (initially ~20–200 employees).
- Secondary: Forex CRM and client portal technology providers.
- Decision-makers: Head of Product, Product Director, COO, or founder.

## Core Offer — €3,000
**Timeline:** Approximately 2–3 weeks per project.

**Deliverables:**
1. UX audit of up to **10 agreed portal screens** and **3 key user journeys** (e.g., dashboard, deposits/withdrawals, account management).
2. Audit report with annotated screenshots, issues, severity, and recommendations.
3. Benchmark against **2–3 accessible competitor experiences**.
4. Prioritized improvement backlog, with suggested effort and selected acceptance criteria.
5. **Figma redesign** of 2 high-impact screens or 1 contained workflow.
6. Suggested **90-day improvement roadmap**.
7. Final presentation and one feedback round.

**Boundaries:** No full portal rebuild, production coding, regulatory audit, or guaranteed conversion uplift. Larger scopes are quoted separately.

## Other Pricing Options
- **Focused UX Audit:** €800–€1,200 for one workflow, findings, and priorities.
- **Expanded Optimization:** €3,500–€5,000+ for broader audits and additional design work.

## Value to the Broker
- Identify friction in critical client workflows.
- Potentially reduce avoidable support requests and task confusion.
- Give internal teams a clear, actionable UX improvement plan.
- Prioritize changes without commissioning a complete redesign.

## Customer Acquisition
1. Build a concise service website and an original sample audit/case study.
2. Research brokers with an existing portal and signs of product improvement needs.
3. Reach Heads of Product, COOs, and founders via personalized email, LinkedIn, or calls.
4. Share **1–2 specific observations** from legitimately accessible interfaces as a mini-audit.
5. Book discovery calls and sell the fixed-scope audit or a smaller paid pilot.

## Business Goal
Reach **€2,000–€3,000 in gross monthly revenue**, initially by closing approximately **one €3,000 project per month**. Revenue will vary with deal flow and delivery capacity.

## Important Considerations
Keep client work separate from current employment; check conflict-of-interest and confidentiality obligations. Use authorized portal access and avoid proprietary employer information. Start solo, keep scope tight, and collect permission-based testimonials and case studies from completed projects.
