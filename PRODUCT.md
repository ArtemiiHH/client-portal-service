# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React.js, Vite, CSS Modules, Phosphor icons, reusable components, responsive layouts, accessible interactions.

## Users
Heads of Product, Product Directors, COOs, and founders of small and mid-sized Forex/CFD brokers. Secondary audience: Forex CRM and client portal technology providers.

## Product Purpose
Convert relevant visitors into discovery calls for a fixed-scope client portal UX audit and improvement roadmap that supports existing product, design, and development teams.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One public package: Client Portal UX Audit & Improvement Roadmap, €2,500 per project, approximately 2–3 weeks. Up to 10 screens and 3 critical journeys; annotated findings with severity and recommendations; benchmark of 2–3 accessible competitor experiences; prioritized backlog with suggested effort and selected acceptance criteria; Figma redesign of 2 high-impact screens or 1 contained workflow; suggested 90-day roadmap; final presentation and one feedback round.

Production implementation, full portal rebuilds, regulatory audits, and guaranteed conversion improvements are excluded. Larger scopes may be discussed privately; smaller and expanded package price ranges are not displayed at launch.

The single scrolling page has exactly five main sections: Hero, How It Works, What You Get, Pricing, and Final Call to Action. Minimal header and footer sit outside these sections. No separate About, Services, or Pricing pages, long FAQ, blog feed, or complex contact form.

## Brand Commitments
The user-authorized restructure replaces the previous design. The pinned foundation is near-black #080A0D, charcoal #151A22, soft lime #D7F871, off-white #F5F6F7, and muted gray #929AA5. Keep the tone restrained, credible, industry-specific, and concise, with spacious typography and subtle glass surfaces.

## Evidence on Hand
BrokerUX-Website-Restructure.md is the current implementation brief. Earlier INSTRUCTIONS.md and broker-client-portal-ux-service-description.md provide background where consistent with it. No client credentials, testimonials, logos, or legitimate sample deliverables have been supplied. Do not invent evidence or outcomes.

## Open Decisions
Booking URL, contact email, LinkedIn URL, and privacy-policy URL are configurable in src/siteConfig.js. Unconfigured booking uses a clearly marked in-page placeholder; email and LinkedIn are omitted unless configured, and privacy displays a coming-soon label until configured. Do not fabricate contact details or legal identity.

## Product Principles
Keep scope and price explicit. Support internal teams. Make deliverables tangible and actionable. Use one discovery-call destination across all conversion CTAs. Distinguish potential benefits from guaranteed outcomes.
